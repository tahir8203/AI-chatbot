# Exams and Attendance

## Attendance rule

Students must attend at least 75% of the classes in each course. Students below 75% attendance are not allowed to sit the final exam for that course and receive an F grade. Medical leave counts as present when a doctor's certificate is submitted within three days.

## Grading

Each course is graded out of 100: assignments and quizzes 25, midterm exam 25, and final exam 50. A score of 85 or above is an A, 70 to 84 is a B, 60 to 69 is a C, 50 to 59 is a D, and below 50 is an F.

## Exam schedule

Midterm exams are held in week 8 and final exams in week 16 of each semester. The date sheet is published on the student portal two weeks before exams begin.

## Missed exams

A student who misses a midterm or final exam for a genuine reason, such as illness or a family emergency, can apply for a make-up exam within three days. The application needs supporting documents and a fee of 3,000 PKR.

## Result and rechecking

Results are published on the student portal within three weeks after final exams. Students can request rechecking of a final exam within seven days of the result for a fee of 1,000 PKR per course.
