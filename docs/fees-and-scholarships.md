# Fees and Scholarships

## Semester fee

The BS tuition fee is 65,000 PKR per semester. The MS tuition fee is 80,000 PKR per semester. A one-time admission fee of 15,000 PKR is paid with the first semester fee.

## Payment and installments

Fees can be paid at any branch of the partner bank or through online banking using the fee voucher from the student portal. Students can pay the semester fee in two installments: 60% before classes start and 40% before the midterm exams. A fine of 100 PKR per day applies after the due date.

## Merit scholarships

Students with a semester GPA of 3.7 or higher receive a 50% tuition waiver for the next semester. Students with a GPA between 3.5 and 3.69 receive a 25% waiver. Merit scholarships are applied automatically; no application is needed.

## Need-based scholarships

Students who cannot afford the full fee can apply for a need-based scholarship through the student portal within the first three weeks of the semester. Applicants must upload proof of family income. Awards cover 25% to 100% of tuition.

## Refund policy

Students who withdraw in the first week of the semester get a 100% tuition refund. Withdrawal in the second week gives a 50% refund. No refund is given after the second week. The admission fee is not refundable.
