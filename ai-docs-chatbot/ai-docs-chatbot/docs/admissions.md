# Admissions

Riverside Institute of Technology is a fictional institute used for this demo.

## Application deadlines

Applications for the Fall semester open on 1 June and close on 15 August. Applications for the Spring semester open on 1 November and close on 15 January. Late applications are accepted for two weeks after the deadline with a late fee of 2,000 PKR.

## Eligibility

Applicants for BS programs need at least 50% marks in Intermediate (FSc, ICS or equivalent). Applicants for MS programs need a 16-year degree with a CGPA of at least 2.5 out of 4 or 60% marks. All applicants must pass the institute entry test.

## Entry test

The entry test has 100 multiple-choice questions: 40 on mathematics, 30 on English and 30 on analytical reasoning. The passing score is 50. Test results are sent by email within five working days.

## Required documents

Upload scanned copies of your CNIC or B-Form, matric and intermediate certificates, two passport-size photos, and the entry test result. MS applicants must also upload their bachelor's transcript and degree.
